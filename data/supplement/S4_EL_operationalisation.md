# Supplementary Material S4 — Proposed LMS indicators for the Ehrman and Leaver (E&L) dichotomies

**Status: uncalibrated proposals. These indicators are NOT inputs to the forecaster.**

Manuscript SSHO-D-26-10943 (R1). In the version first submitted, the list below stood in the PPSM subsection of the model-architecture section. In the revision it was moved here in response to Reviewer 7 (points 5 and 6), so that the paper keeps descriptive information apart from the forecaster's predictive inputs. The text of the list is reproduced verbatim from the submitted manuscript (LaTeX markup converted to Markdown).

- The forecaster's input vector is specified in the section "Mathematical formulation of forecasting" of the paper. The E&L dichotomies are not among its inputs. They are held in the descriptive layer of the Psychological-Pedagogical Student Model (PPSM), for the student and the teacher to discuss.
- None of the indicators below has been calibrated against the explicit E&L instrument, and the transfer of the E&L framework beyond second-language learning, where it was formulated, has not been established. Calibration is one element of the paper's validation protocol (section "Validation protocol").
- The indicators are therefore proposals awaiting calibration, not measurements of a validated construct.

## Proposed indicators (verbatim from the submitted manuscript)

- **Random / Sequential** is approached by measuring the linearity of navigation. The indicators are sequence-adherence in module access, the variance of module-completion order from the prescribed sequence, the order in which a student opens course resources, and the time the student spends on non-sequential rather than sequential navigation paths.
- **Levelling / Sharpening** is read off iterative work. We track the pattern of error correction in iterative assignments, the frequency and magnitude of revisions between submission attempts, the speed and fidelity with which feedback is incorporated, and how the initial and final versions of an assignment differ.
- **Abstract / Concrete** is read off content preference. The indicators are the preferred resource type (theoretical material versus applied examples), the approach taken on assignments (formula derivation versus numerical problem solving), the abstraction level of contributions to discussion (text-mining-based), and the time the student allocates to conceptual versus procedural materials.
- **Field (in)dependence / field sensitivity** is read off attention. We use clickstream analysis to measure selective attention in multimedia materials, the breadth-versus-depth ratio in navigation, susceptibility to off-task navigation, and the student's ability to filter irrelevant elements in complex learning interfaces.
- **Ectenic / Synoptic** is read off the way the student handles support. The indicators are the frequency and timing of help-system use, the rate of step-by-step tutorial completion versus direct task engagement, the distribution of time-to-solution, and the frequency of hint requests during problem-solving.

Closing sentence of the submitted passage (verbatim): "We emphasise that these indicators are proposed; their psychometric validity against the explicit E&L instrument is one of the items that empirical validation has to settle."
