# Full-text decision rules

The main full-text assessor wrote these rules into the header of its progress log before it assessed its first record, and later wrote down one clarification (at record 34, applied from record 1). Both are reproduced verbatim below. The adjudicator was told to judge against the same rules ([prompt 04](04_adjudication.md), "Additions"); the later full-text assessors were given them by message, which is not reproduced (see README.md in this folder). The assessor of the last worklist (manualB) added rules of its own in the header of its log, reproduced verbatim in the last section; the adjudicator upheld its E4/NOREL tie-break and applied it to all records ([prompt 05](05_adjudicator_notes_on_rules.md), section 11, "Rule I").

## Rules (verbatim)

Decision rules (fixed before record 1, applied to all records):
- include: a per-student, platform-recorded measure or learning-activity product enters an analysis against a learning-action success measure or a personality-related construct. Group membership (e.g. blended vs traditional) is not an indicator.
- E4: the only quantitative result is an achievement comparison, and no activity measure enters any analysis.
- NOREL: an LMS/activity measure exists but is never empirically tied to success or a construct, or all data are self-report (pure surveys land here).
- E1: only when nothing at all is given from which reliability could be judged.
- E3: only clearly non-HE/VET samples; residents/trainees decided on other criteria, population noted.
- Precedence when several apply: E6 > E2 > NONEMP > E3 > E4 > NOREL > E1; secondary codes go in extraction.notes.

## Clarification (verbatim)

Rule clarification (applied from record 1, written down at record 34): intervention/group-comparison designs whose outcomes include achievement and in which no per-student activity measure is analysed -> E4 (even if self-report scales are also outcomes); designs whose outcomes are only self-report -> NOREL; designs where a platform activity measure is recorded/analysed but never related to success or a construct -> NOREL.

## Rules added by the assessor of worklist manualB (verbatim)

Added for this shard, fixed before record 1:
- An LMS-recorded quiz/test/exam score is an achievement outcome, not an indicator. It counts as an indicator only when a per-student formative-activity record or product (quiz attempts, assignment/submission products, completion counts) enters an analysis against a separate success measure or a construct. A between-group comparison of test scores alone is E4.
- Off-domain texts (no education content) are coded NOREL with "off-domain" in the reason (no OFF code at full text).
- E6 is judged on the body language of the full text (Russian body = E6; Ukrainian or English = eligible).
- Tie-break E4 vs NOREL (added after the first pass, applied to all records): when no per-student platform/activity measure enters any analysis and an achievement or performance measure is among the compared outcomes, code E4 even if self-report scales are also compared (E4 > NOREL); code NOREL when outcomes are self-report only, or when a platform measure exists but is never related to an outcome.
