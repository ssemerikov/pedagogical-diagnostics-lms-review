# PRISMA 2020 checklist

Status: **complete**. The column "Location in the article" gives, for each item, where it is reported in the revised
article as submitted in November 2026 (sections and subsections by title; tables and figures by number), or states
"Not reported" where the article does not contain it. The column "In this repository" points to the material here
that supports an item.

The checklist items are reproduced verbatim from Table 1 (and, for item 2, Table 2) of Page MJ, McKenzie JE,
Bossuyt PM, Boutron I, Hoffmann TC, Mulrow CD, et al. The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for
reporting systematic reviews. *BMJ* 2021;372:n71. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71, distributed under the
Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) licence.

The review is reported as an updated review: the original OUCI search (January 2026) and the update (Scopus and
an OUCI re-run, November 2026) are reported separately, as the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram for updated reviews
provides.
